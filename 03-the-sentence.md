# The completed sentence as *unitus*
[Home](index.html) · [Previous](02-structure-and-meaning.md) · [Next](04-unfolding-and-landing.md)

The sentence is a revealing **unitus**: a locally completed act of intelligibility whose unfolding can often be examined word by word. Its scale allows a governing determination to be reconstructed.

The unit is functional rather than merely orthographic. A fragment can complete a conversational demand; a sentence may contain subordinate local completions; and a single sentence can support several governing interpretations in different contexts.

Therefore the investigational unit is best understood as **a sentence under a specified intelligible demand**, not a string considered in isolation.
