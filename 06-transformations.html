Six Transformation Patterns

Home · Previous · Next

The Transformation Pattern asks what governing insufficiency is overcome?

PatternGoverning transformation
DifferentiationAn unavailable or inoperative difference becomes determinate enough to matter.
OpeningAccess, reach, availability or possible continuation becomes newly possible.
RelatingA dependency, correspondence, ordering or reciprocal bearing becomes constitutive.
RepairAn organization failing relative to an operative criterion ceases to be governed by that misfit.
IntegrationParts acquire determinate roles through their organization into a whole.
SettlementA live demand for choice, explanation, uptake or continuation is discharged.

Opening differs from Repair by the latter's criterion of fault. Relating differs from Integration by the latter's whole-making organization. Settlement can discharge a demand without repairing anything.

These definitions are concise reconstructions of the canonical controlling reference.
