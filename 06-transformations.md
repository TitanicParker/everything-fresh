# Six Transformation Patterns
[Home](index.html) · [Previous](05-operative-centre.md) · [Next](07-topologies.md)

The Transformation Pattern asks **what governing insufficiency is overcome?**

| Pattern | Governing transformation |
|---|---|
| **Differentiation** | An unavailable or inoperative difference becomes determinate enough to matter. |
| **Opening** | Access, reach, availability or possible continuation becomes newly possible. |
| **Relating** | A dependency, correspondence, ordering or reciprocal bearing becomes constitutive. |
| **Repair** | An organization failing relative to an operative criterion ceases to be governed by that misfit. |
| **Integration** | Parts acquire determinate roles through their organization into a whole. |
| **Settlement** | A live demand for choice, explanation, uptake or continuation is discharged. |

Opening differs from Repair by the latter's criterion of fault. Relating differs from Integration by the latter's whole-making organization. Settlement can discharge a demand without repairing anything.

These definitions are concise reconstructions of the [canonical controlling reference](https://github.com/TitanicParker/fiche-ceathar/blob/main/THE_54_CONTROL.md).
