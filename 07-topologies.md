# Three Completion Topologies
[Home](index.html) · [Previous](06-transformations.md) · [Next](08-persistence.md)

Completion Topology asks **what organization makes the governing transformation sufficient?**

| Topology | Sufficiency condition |
|---|---|
| **Focal** | One privileged locus is constitutive of the completion. |
| **Distributed** | A constitutive plurality, sequence, span or field is indispensable. |
| **Release** | A formerly governing constraint loses its determining force. |

Release is not mere negation. The previous governor must be identifiable and shown to have lost authority.

**Unresolved structural issue:** Focal and Distributed appear to describe the locus of sufficiency, whereas Release describes a change in governance. A release can also be focal or distributed. The canonical grammar treats them as rival *governing conditions*, but this requires independent testing.
