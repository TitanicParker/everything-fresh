# The 54: complete atlas of operative characters
[Home](index.html) · [Persistence](08-persistence.md) · [Sentence laboratory](10-sentence-laboratory.md)

The 54 are the proposed conjunction of **six Transformation Patterns × three Completion Topologies × three Persistence Modes**. The dimensions coincide in one achieved event; they are not assembled sequentially.

**Status:** Canonical names are inherited from the [controlling reference](https://github.com/TitanicParker/fiche-ceathar/blob/main/THE_54_CONTROL.md). The operative characters below are **constructive derivations**, not independently validated unique realizations. A coordinate constrains a family of possible centres, not necessarily one image.

## Interpretation
A form is the repeatable **constraint on a kind of sufficient completion**. A concrete realization is the particular governing dependency in a sentence under a specified demand. The canonical name is a memorable crystallization, not proof of uniqueness.

**Neighbour tests:** Repair requires a criterion of fault; Opening requires newly available possibility; Settlement discharges a live demand. Focal requires one privileged locus; Distributed requires a constitutive plurality; Release requires loss of an identifiable former governor. Standing, Bearing and Efficacy distinguish what remains achieved.

**Open objection:** Release may cross-cut Focal and Distributed; the grammar's claim of exclusive governing conditions remains to be tested.
