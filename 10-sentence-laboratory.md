These are **constructed analyses**, not blind-trial confirmations. Each has a governing demand and a rival.

### “The shelf holds weight because its parts brace one another.”
The first clause asserts capacity; the explanatory clause supplies its indispensable organization. **Centre:** joint efficacy through distributed supporting roles. **Candidate:** Integration × Distributed × Efficacy (42). **Rival:** Cell 41, if mutual support itself is the achievement. **Contrast:** “The parts brace one another, but the shelf cannot hold weight.”

### “The bicycle can pass now that the last box has moved.”
The late clause supplies the condition making the earlier opening claim sufficient. **Centre:** a barrier loses governing force. **Candidate:** Opening × Release × Efficacy (18). **Rival:** Repair × Release × Efficacy (36) if the obstruction violated a functional standard. **Contrast:** “Two boxes moved, but the bicycle still cannot pass.”

### “The red square stands out against the grey ones.”
A focal difference becomes available through contrast. **Candidate:** Differentiation × Focal × Bearing (02). **Rival:** Differentiation × Distributed × Bearing (05), if the governing achievement is a field of context-sensitive distinctions.

### “The machine stopped rattling after the loose bolt was tightened.”
A fault is corrected and effective operation restored. **Candidate:** Repair × Release × Efficacy (36). **Rival:** Settlement × Release × Efficacy (54), if the demand is simply whether the rattling stopped. **Contrast:** “The machine stopped rattling because the power failed.”

### “Each adjustment changes the next, and the last changes the first.”
The final clause retrospectively closes the dependency chain into a cycle. **Candidate:** Relating × Distributed × Efficacy (24). **Rival:** Relating × Distributed × Bearing (23) if the achievement is mutual dependency, not an operative cycle.

### “The old rule no longer decides who may enter; actual need does.”
A previous classification loses authority; a new discriminating criterion governs selection. **Candidate:** Differentiation × Release × Efficacy (09). **Rival:** Repair × Release × Efficacy (36) if the governing demand is to correct an unjust system.

### “Now it makes sense.”
Without context, this does not reveal what changed or why. Cell 53 is plausible for retrospective explanatory settlement, but **abstention** is more rigorous than a forced assignment.

### “The horse raced past the barn fell.”
The final verb forces reanalysis of “raced past the barn” as a reduced relative. The governing dependency is distributed across the sentence; a unique 54 coordinate is not determined without an interpretive demand.

### “Tomorrow.”
A fragment in isolation; a complete answer to “When is the hearing?” The unit of intelligibility is functional, not simply grammatical.

**Method:** reconstruct prefix changes; freeze the governing unresolvedness, decisive dependency, sufficiency condition and achieved result; then compare the strongest one-axis rival. Do not label first.
