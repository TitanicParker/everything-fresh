This is a selective intellectual reconstruction, not a complete dated audit of all historical repositories.

Early sentence landing: How Sentences Come to Rest examined how unfolding possibilities become settled. The Atlas of Remainders sought stable invariants and excluded rival landings.

Early 54 geometry: six Works (Distinction, Expansion, Relation, Correction, Integration, Rest), three Orientations (Toward, Across, Away), and three Survivors (Is, With, Does).

Mature constraint grammar: Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode became the governing vocabulary. The retrospective documents the reversal from reading coordinates out of sentences to generating admissible forms from coordinates, while distinguishing canonical crystallization from concrete realization.

Blind sentence work: GPT-A blind assay freezes the unfolding sentence analysis before later coordinate assignment.

Operative Centre: the current proposal joins history-first reconstruction with grammar-first constraint. Neither direction replaces the other.
