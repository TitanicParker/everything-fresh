This is a **selective intellectual reconstruction**, not a complete dated audit of all historical repositories.

**Early sentence landing:** [How Sentences Come to Rest](https://github.com/TitanicParker/how-sentences-come-to-rest) examined how unfolding possibilities become settled. The [Atlas of Remainders](https://github.com/TitanicParker/how-sentences-come-to-rest/blob/main/books/02-atlas-of-remainders/README.md) sought stable invariants and excluded rival landings.

**Early 54 geometry:** six Works (Distinction, Expansion, Relation, Correction, Integration, Rest), three Orientations (Toward, Across, Away), and three Survivors (Is, With, Does).

**Mature constraint grammar:** Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode became the governing vocabulary. The [retrospective](https://github.com/TitanicParker/persistence/blob/main/constraint_grammar_from_sentence_resolution_to_constraint_grammar_intellectual_transformation.md) documents the reversal from reading coordinates out of sentences to generating admissible forms from coordinates, while distinguishing canonical crystallization from concrete realization.

**Blind sentence work:** [GPT-A blind assay](https://github.com/TitanicParker/fiche-ceathar/blob/main/protocols/GPT_A_BLIND_SENTENCE_ASSAY.md) freezes the unfolding sentence analysis before later coordinate assignment.

**Operative Centre:** the current proposal joins history-first reconstruction with grammar-first constraint. Neither direction replaces the other.
