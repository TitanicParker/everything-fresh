# Experimental recognition protocol
[Home](index.html) · [History](11-history.md) · [Glossary](13-glossary.md)

1. **Completion gate:** Decide whether the supplied text and context form a sufficient local act; refuse if materially unfinished.
2. **Blind unfolding:** Record prefix-by-prefix live interpretations, constraints, local closures and retrospective changes; hide the cell names.
3. **Freeze the Centre:** Specify unresolved demand, indispensable dependency, sufficiency condition and achieved result.
4. **Rival:** Identify the strongest competing centre and a minimal discriminating contrast.
5. **Delayed axes:** Reveal the fifteen definitions and derive Transformation, Topology and Persistence as one event.
6. **Neighbour audit:** Compare the nearest one-axis rival; abstain when evidence is insufficient.
7. **Independent replication:** Compare fresh investigators on the governing dependency before comparing coordinates.

A proper test preregisters unseen ordinary and difficult sentences, fragments, garden paths, paraphrases, minimal pairs and ambiguous contexts. Compare axis-first, centre-first and coordinate-free constraint-transition methods. Blind adjudicators to the intended coordinate. Record abstentions and disagreements.

**Failure conditions:** post-hoc centres that merely paraphrase labels; instability under faithful paraphrase; inability to distinguish nearest rivals; a simpler rival grammar with superior explanatory performance.

The [internal short-form experiment](https://github.com/TitanicParker/making-sense--together/blob/main/SHORT_FORM_EXPERIMENT.md) concerns curated cues; it is not independent confirmation of the Operative Centre.
