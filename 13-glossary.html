Glossary

Home · Experiments · Sources

Intelligibility: relational organization sufficiently determinate for uptake.

Unitus: a locally completed act of intelligibility.

Governing demand: the unresolved question or pressure relative to which a change can be enough.

Landing: the point or span where governing organization becomes sufficient.

Operative Centre: the minimal governing determination through which unfolding intelligibility becomes sufficient and holds as achieved; not a fourth axis.

Transformation Pattern: the governing insufficiency overcome.

Completion Topology: the structural condition that makes the overcoming sufficient.

Persistence Mode: the manner in which the achievement remains available.

Dimensional Conjunction: reciprocal determination of the three axes in one completion-event.

Canonical crystallization: a stabilized semantic name for a coordinate-generated form, not necessarily its only realization.

Concrete realization: the particular governing resolution in an actual sentence.

Release: loss of determining force by a prior governor, not simple negation.

Abstention: refusal to assign a unique form where evidence or context is inadequate.
