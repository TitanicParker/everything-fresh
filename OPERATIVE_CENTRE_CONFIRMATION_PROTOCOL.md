# Operative Centre Confirmation and the One-Way Threshold

**Working resolution protocol — 20 October 2026.** This procedure supplements the workbook. The canonical definitions and 54-cell coordinates in [THE_54_CONTROL.md](THE_54_CONTROL.md) remain unchanged.

**Governing direction:** The sentence determines the Operative Centre. The confirmed Centre determines the cell. A proposed cell never determines the Centre retrospectively.

## Gate One — Identify and confirm the Operative Centre

Work with the source sentence **without consulting cell names, the cell lookup, or anticipated coordinates**.

1. **Source boundary.** Record the exact sentence, corpus ID and version. Preserve qualifications, modality, negation, scope and essential dependencies.
2. **Unfolding.** Trace what remains unresolved, how the sentence's contributions develop, and what governing determination makes the entire unfolding sufficient.
3. **Candidate Centre.** State the *smallest complete governing determination*, not a subject summary, theme or attractive paraphrase.
4. **Verification.** Test deletion, alteration, narrowing and reversal of indispensable contributions. Ask whether the proposed Centre still accounts for the original completed achievement. Remove invented causal claims, purposes or implications.
5. **Structural determinacy.** Answer the six questions below **in ordinary, pre-taxonomic language**, as features of the same achieved intelligibility.

| Confirmation question | Required determination |
|---|---|
| What was unresolved? | The specific demand that completion overcomes |
| What changed? | The determination achieved by the whole utterance |
| What made it sufficient? | The indispensable constitutive organisation |
| Where is sufficiency located? | The locus or combination necessary for completion |
| What remains achieved? | The exact identity of what is left achieved, including what must remain constitutive for it to hold as *this* completion (see the holding test below) |
| What would invalidate it? | The smallest change that defeats that achievement |

### Sharpened holding test — complete before freezing

The question **“What remains achieved?”** must do more than name the outcome or say that it continues. While the sentence is still open, determine **what the achievement consists in once it is complete**. Write the answer without canonical axis terms or cell names.

Answer these four prompts as a single description of the Centre:

1. **Identity:** What exactly has been established? Distinguish a completed determination, an achievement constituted by its dependence on something else, and an achieved way something can proceed or operate — *in ordinary language, without selecting among taxonomic categories*.
2. **Constitutive dependency:** If a stated condition, contrast, support, or relation were no longer part of the achievement, would the *identity of the completed result* change, or would only its description or evidential support change? Say precisely which.
3. **Operative continuation:** Does the achieved result itself determine what can subsequently happen, how something can operate, or how a continuation proceeds? If yes, describe the exact consequence established. **Do not infer a capacity merely because a statement is a rule, could be applied, or is practically useful.**
4. **Minimal invalidation:** What smallest change would make the *completed achievement itself* no longer hold? Distinguish loss of the result from loss of the relation constituting it or loss of its operative consequence.

### Achieved-identity discrimination — established result or constitutive bearing

**Use this additional test during Gate One, before the source sentence is closed.** It sharpens identification; it does not change the canonical definitions. Do **not** introduce the labels Standing, Bearing, any cell number, or anticipated classification while carrying out the test.

A relationship, condition, comparison, or plurality of indispensable terms can be necessary to *form* a complete determination without making the achieved identity consist in an ongoing determination of one term **through** another. Necessity of the terms alone is not the discriminant.

Write both formulations in ordinary language:

- **Result formulation:** “What has been established is ___.”
- **Constitution formulation:** “What obtains through what is ___.”

Then test each formulation against the *complete governing achievement* rather than the grammatical ease of writing it:

1. **Completion versus constitution:** Does the full achievement consist in the established determination as a result, or is what has been determined itself the constitutive bearing of terms through one another? Specify the exact difference without dropping qualifications.
2. **Identity rather than prerequisites:** Which dependencies are indispensable **to identifying what the completed result is**, rather than merely being prerequisites for establishing or describing it? Do not infer constitutive bearing solely because removing a term also removes the result: that counterfactual defeats many kinds of completion.
3. **Rival formulation test:** Does either formulation omit part of the completed achievement, or does one invent a dependence not established by the source? If both remain adequate, record the exact difference and pursue further pre-freeze tests; do not manufacture uniqueness by paraphrase.
4. **Holding statement:** State positively *what remains achieved* and *why its identity holds that way*, so the frozen record can discriminate an established relational configuration from a determination constituted through the terms' bearing.

**Illustrative discrimination, not a lookup or automatic classification:** An established temporal lead between two arrivals may be achieved *as the ordered configuration itself*, even though both arrivals are required to describe the lead. An exact quantitative determination of one magnitude through specified counterparts may instead have that quantitative dependence as the identity of what is achieved. In either case the source analysis, not the illustration or the words “temporal” and “quantitative,” must decide.

This test **never treats relational syntax as sufficient for constitutive bearing**, and never treats the mere fact that a relationship is established as sufficient to exclude such bearing. If the resulting Centre remains genuinely neutral, preserve that distinction rather than silently rewriting it after confirmation.

**Required final line in Gate One:** “What remains achieved is ___; it holds as this achievement because ___; removing ___ would change/defeat it in the following way: ___.”

The record must distinguish the **constitution of completion** (what makes it sufficient) from the **identity of what remains achieved** (what persists as the completed result). Do not mistake the presence of multiple terms for a distributed completion, a relational sentence for relation-dependent holding, or the description of motion/change for an achieved operative consequence. These distinctions must come from the Centre analysis, not from a preview of the 54 cells.

If two materially different accounts of what remains achieved still fit, continue testing them **before** confirmation. Do not leave the choice to cell selection after the One-Way Threshold.

Before confirming, check **coverage** (nothing governing omitted), **minimality** (nothing unnecessary included), **non-invention** (nothing unsupported added), and **determinacy** (one complete governing achievement described).

### Centre Confirmation Record — required fields

- Sentence ID and source version (provenance only).
- **Confirmed Operative Centre:** one exact, minimally sufficient formulation.
- **Opening constraint:** the unresolvedness.
- **Achieved completion:** what is made sufficient.
- **Constitutive organisation:** how the achievement becomes sufficient.
- **Location of sufficiency:** what is constitutive of its completion.
- **Mode of holding:** the completed holding-test line, specifying the result's identity, what remains constitutive of that identity, and the minimal change that defeats it.
- **Decisive counterfactual:** the smallest variation that would defeat it.
- **Confirmation:** coverage, minimality, determinacy and non-invention check.

The confirmation record must be self-sufficient for cell derivation. Its descriptions must arise from the source analysis, **not** from the vocabulary of the canonical dimensions.

When the record passes Gate One, write exactly:

**OPERATIVE CENTRE CONFIRMED — SOURCE SENTENCE CLOSED.**

## The One-Way Threshold

**After confirmation, do not reread, quote, paraphrase, amend or appeal to the original sentence to decide a dimension or cell.** The classifier works only from the frozen Centre Confirmation Record and the canonical reference.

A dispute between cells is a question about the structure of the **confirmed Centre**, not an invitation to select a phrase from the source sentence to decide between them. Do not retrofit the Centre to match a candidate.

## Gate Two — Determine the unique cell

1. Take the complete frozen Centre Confirmation Record as the sole sentence-specific input.
2. Consult [THE_54_CONTROL.md](THE_54_CONTROL.md). Determine the unified completion under **simultaneous** Transformation Pattern × Completion Topology × Persistence Mode. The dimensions are not successive operations or independent descriptive labels.
3. Eliminate incompatible cells by reference to **specific constraints of the Centre**, not resemblance to cell names or individual words.
4. Compare the strongest surviving neighbouring cells: which demands a different constitution of sufficiency, governing transformation or mode of holding?
5. Identify the **single surviving cell**, giving number, canonical name, conjunction, and the structural reasons alternatives fail. If more than one survives, specify the exact unresolved structural distinction in the frozen record rather than making a preference-based selection.

**The desired result is a Centre determinate enough to exclude the other 53 candidates.** The one-way threshold ensures the dimensional identity is derived from that Centre alone.
