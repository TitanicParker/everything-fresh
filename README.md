# Everything Fresh — The Operative Centre of Intelligibility

How does a sentence become one intelligible whole?

A completed sentence is a *unitus* of intelligibility. Its **Operative Centre** is the minimal governing determination through which its unfolding organization becomes sufficient and holds as achieved.

The 54 are the proposed grammar of **six Transformation Patterns × three Completion Topologies × three Persistence Modes**. The Centre is not a fourth axis or necessarily a single word. Canonical cell names and concrete sentence realizations must be distinguished.
