# Everything Fresh — An exhibition of the Operative Centre method

**How does a sentence become one intelligible whole?** A completed sentence is a *unitus* of intelligibility. Its **Operative Centre** is the minimal governing determination through which unfolding organization becomes sufficient and holds as achieved.

This repository exhibits a **research method under test**. The 54 are a proposed grammar of six Transformation Patterns × three Completion Topologies × three Persistence Modes, not a demonstrated exhaustive or uniquely recoverable classification of all sentences.
