Workbook-first research workflow

The repository is an exhibition of a method under test, not a declaration that every sentence has a unique 54-cell identity.

Authorities and records

  1. Canonical vocabulary: THE54CONTROL.md controls the 15 definitions, Dimensional Conjunction, numbering and crystallizations until an explicit versioned change.
  2. Working synthesis: OPERATIVECENTREWORKBOOK200SENTENCES.html is the complete HTML exhibition of the uploaded root Markdown workbook, which is the research source of record.
  3. Frozen corpora: Sections 15 and 16 of the unified workbook contain both complete frozen corpora, with stable IDs A-001…A-100 and B-001…B-100. Do not silently rewrite a sentence after freezing.
  4. Experimental results: laboratory/RESOLUTION_REGISTER.md tracks candidate resolutions and their revisions separately from source sentences.
  5. Exhibition: index.html and the numbered chapters explain and demonstrate the method. Sentence receipts present each completed corpus sentence, its current resolution and commentary on how the structure and canonical name show themselves. Each receipt links to a full HTML diligence page rendered from the immutable research record. The public surface must follow the latest indexed revision and retain candidate/contested/revised status.

Delegated corpus stewardship

[Fresher's 200-sentence handover and direct-write mandate](laboratory/FRESHER_STEWARDSHIP.md) governs the one-sentence-at-a-time programme, persistent per-sentence notes, resumption, and permission to write ordinary corpus records directly to the repository when authorised GitHub tools are connected.

Resolution protocol

Start here: Operative Centre Confirmation and the One-Way Threshold is the operative step-by-step protocol for fresh GPT tests.

  1. Retrieve the exact source sentence and establish its governing achievement without using the 54-cell lookup.
  2. Apply the six Centre confirmation questions: what was unresolved, what changed, what made completion sufficient, where sufficiency is located, what remains achieved, and what would invalidate it.
  3. Sharpen “What remains achieved?” before freezing: identify the result's continuing identity, constitutive dependencies, operative consequences (only where actually established), and the smallest change that defeats it. Apply the achieved-identity discrimination: write both “What has been established is __” and “What obtains through what is __,” then determine which actually captures the governing achievement (rather than relying on relational wording or indispensable terms). Verify minimality, coverage, non-invention and determinacy. Freeze the complete Centre Confirmation Record.
  4. Declare OPERATIVE CENTRE CONFIRMED — SOURCE SENTENCE CLOSED. Do not reopen the source sentence during classification.
  5. From the frozen record alone, apply Dimensional Conjunction using the canonical reference; eliminate incompatible cells by structural contradiction, not semantic resemblance.
  6. Derive the strongest surviving cell and test nearest rivals against the frozen Centre. If the record does not discriminate, name the exact boundary rather than making a preference-based choice.
  7. Preserve each record and any later revisions as separate dated entries; never silently overwrite the frozen Centre.
  8. Publish the receipt after persistence: add or update the sentence in sentence-receipts.html with exact source wording, current cell/conjunction or surviving alternatives, status, and plain-language paragraphs showing how the structure and its canonical name evidence themselves. Render the current full record to exhibition/diligence/{ID}.html, link it from the receipt, and refresh RESOLUTION_REGISTER.html. On revision, the receipt points to the current result while earlier diligence stays available. Verify the public links and source correspondence before reporting completion.

Status vocabulary

  • Unclassified: source sentence has no completed assay.
  • Centre frozen: Stage One preserved before taxonomy.
  • Candidate: Stage Two coordinates proposed.
  • Contested: material rival or independent disagreement.
  • Revised: later analysis changes a previous candidate.
  • Abstained: insufficient grounds to assign a cell.

Change discipline

  • Keep original corpus text immutable and use sentence IDs in every record.
  • Preserve old analytical conclusions and their timestamps.
  • Distinguish canonical definitions from working hypotheses and illustrative crystallizations.
  • Treat the 54 as a testable proposed grammar, not an established exhaustive partition.
  • Prefer reviewed pull requests for changes to the workbook, corpus and canonical mappings.
