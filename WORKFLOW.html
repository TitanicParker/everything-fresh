Workbook-first research workflow

The repository is an exhibition of a method under test, not a declaration that every sentence has a unique 54-cell identity.

Authorities and records

  1. Canonical vocabulary: THE_54_CONTROL.md controls the 15 definitions, Dimensional Conjunction, numbering and crystallizations until an explicit versioned change.
  2. Working synthesis: workbook/OPERATIVE_CENTRE_WORKBOOK.md is the intended consolidated research record. The prepared 200-sentence workbook must be imported and verified before this path becomes authoritative.
  3. Frozen corpora: corpora/A-world-100.md and corpora/B-science-100.md are the intended exact-text source copies, with stable IDs A-001…A-100 and B-001…B-100. Do not silently rewrite a sentence after freezing.
  4. Experimental results: laboratory/RESOLUTION_REGISTER.md tracks candidate resolutions and their revisions separately from source sentences.
  5. Exhibition: index.html and the numbered chapters explain and demonstrate the method. They should link to, not replace, the evidence.

Resolution protocol

  1. Record the exact sentence, corpus ID and source version.
  2. Stage One, without taxonomy: trace unfolding intelligibility; identify the governing demand, decisive dependency, sufficiency condition and minimal Operative Centre. Freeze this record.
  3. Stage Two: apply Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode as simultaneous constraints on the one completion-event; compute candidate cell number.
  4. Test at least the strongest one-axis neighbour, and a cross-pattern rival where relevant.
  5. Use counterfactuals to test the centre and the proposed dimensional distinction. Note when a test cannot discriminate between rivals.
  6. Record confidence, abstention or uniqueness withheld. Do not turn agreement into a claim of verified truth.
  7. If independent assessment is requested, give the other assessor the predeclared permitted inputs, freeze its answer before comparison, then record exact-cell and dimensional agreement.
  8. Corrections are appended with dates, reasons and superseded candidate IDs. Never rewrite a frozen response as though it were original.

Status vocabulary

  • Unclassified: source sentence has no completed assay.
  • Centre frozen: Stage One preserved before taxonomy.
  • Candidate: Stage Two coordinates proposed.
  • Contested: material rival or independent disagreement.
  • Revised: later analysis changes a previous candidate.
  • Abstained: insufficient grounds to assign a cell.

Change discipline

  • Keep original corpus text immutable and use sentence IDs in every record.
  • Preserve old analytical conclusions and their timestamps.
  • Distinguish canonical definitions from working hypotheses and illustrative crystallizations.
  • Treat the 54 as a testable proposed grammar, not an established exhaustive partition.
  • Prefer reviewed pull requests for changes to the workbook, corpus and canonical mappings.

Initial import checklist

  • [ ] Copy the complete prepared 200-sentence workbook to workbook/OPERATIVE_CENTRE_WORKBOOK.md.
  • [ ] Extract both exact source corpora into their intended files without changing text.
  • [ ] Check that A-001…A-100 and B-001…B-100 each appear exactly once.
  • [ ] Confirm all workbook cross-links and update its revision history.
  • [ ] Link the public homepage to the workbook and the two corpora after the files exist.
  • [ ] Add first verified resolution entries; do not pre-populate 200 speculative cells.
