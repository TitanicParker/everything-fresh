Gate One — source analysis and freeze

Exact source, scope and unfolding

Somewhere tonight, a child will learn the moon follows no one home.

A-004, frozen Corpus A §15, Edition 1.1, original item 4; source file One_Hundred_Statements_About_the_World(4).md; workbook blob 1b511a439a5007a3c4a61c36ff5417f415005e2c. Text matched verbatim.

“Somewhere tonight” locates a projected learning event without identifying a location or clock time. It is retained as the source's deictic wording, not turned into a claim about this particular assay date. “A child will learn” predicts an acquisition of knowledge, not an already completed lesson. “The moon follows no one home” specifies what is learned. The universal negative concerns the content; the learning event concerns an unspecified child, not all children.

The ordinary reading denies personal following, not apparent relative position or all possible resemblance of paths. No literal pursuit, astronomical explanation or account of the child's previous belief is supplied.

  1. A possible place and the source's tonight frame are introduced.
  2. An unspecified child's future acquisition is predicted; its content remains unresolved.
  3. The negative content completes the prediction: the child comes to know that the moon is not anyone's follower on a journey home.

The utterance is complete now as a prediction. It does not assert that the projected acquisition has already happened. The achievement within its content is knowledge of non-following, not movement or learning created by the act of utterance.

Four holding tests

  1. Identity. The completed utterance predicts an acquisition of determinate knowledge by a child somewhere in the specified tonight. Within that projected achievement, what is retained is knowledge of the moon's non-following, not merely a newly accessible lesson.
  2. Constitutive dependencies. Learner, future acquisition, content and setting identify the prediction. The knowledge is not claimed to obtain through another term's ongoing support or reciprocal determination. Knowing has an object, but that grammatical/content dependency alone does not establish a further achieved dependent status.
  3. Operative continuation. No ensuing capacity, decision, journey, emotional change or independent procedure is established. The child's future conduct is unspecified.
  4. Minimal invalidation. A child who does not acquire the stated knowledge defeats the projected achievement while leaving the negative fact and temporal frame intact. Changing only what is learned changes the particular achievement. These tests do not imply a previously governing error was removed.

Achieved-identity discrimination

Result formulation: What has been established is the prediction that somewhere tonight a child will acquire the knowledge that the moon follows nobody home.

Constitution formulation: What obtains through what is the child's knowledge of non-following through its grasp of the moon's relation to people going home.

The result formulation retains the complete learner, acquisition, content, quantifiers and temporal projection. The constitution formulation can explain what knowledge is about, but gives no extra stated support, condition or mutual determination through which this knowledge continues to obtain. It risks turning any knowledge-with-content into a special dependency.

Further tests before freezing:

  • Keep the acquisition and content while change the teaching source or remove any assumed teacher: the assertion can remain; an authority or support is not indispensable.
  • Keep the content and remove a previously mistaken belief: learning can still happen, so a corrective identity is not required.
  • Keep the complete projected acquisition but omit any later behaviour: nothing asserted is lost.
  • Treat the no-one scope as known generally rather than acquired by testing people one after another: the achievement remains.

Outcome: The projected knowledge acquisition is retained as a determinate result. A specifically correcting, liberating, supported or practically consequential achievement would add source content. The result remains negative in content, but no previous governor has been identified.
