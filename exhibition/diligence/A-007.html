Four holding tests

  1. Identity: one articulated instructional endowment, constituted by guidance for the full range of named domains.
  2. Constitutive dependencies: the contributions identify the whole. This is compositional constitution, not an assertion that they continually sustain each other or that one domain defines another.
  3. Operative continuation: the content is action-directed, but no further accomplished performance or effective operation is required to identify the whole established here. Lack of execution alone would not exclude capacity; the positive governing point is the complete endowment rather than an additional demonstrated competence.
  4. Minimal invalidation: retain a seed and environmental items but remove their shared instructional organisation: possession and a list can remain while the unified endowment disappears. A one-item deletion instead narrows coverage, not necessarily all wholeness.

Achieved-identity discrimination

Result formulation: What has been established is one seed's unified instructional endowment, articulated across guidance for soil, sunlight, insects and time.

Constitution formulation: What obtains through what is the endowment's character as a whole through the joint contribution of that scoped guidance.

The latter identifies what makes the whole complete. It does not supply a distinct ongoing support relation through which the whole maintains its achieved identity. Composition into the whole is indispensable; that fact is not automatically a different dependent mode of holding.

Further pre-freeze tests distinguish inventory, whole, sustaining structure and operative outcome. An unrelated inventory preserves containment but loses the whole. An intact whole without a specified mutual-support mechanism preserves this achievement. An intact whole without a specified successful operation also preserves it. These tests support the established whole as the retained point.

Learned correction and future diligence

Operational lesson for subsequent assays: identify what the complete utterance makes sufficient before privileging its grammatical verb. “Carries” may present a whole endowment rather than make possession the governing accomplishment. Conversely, “is” does not automatically establish Integration.

Use these checks before freezing future records:

  1. Express the proposed Centre as a completed achievement in ordinary language.
  2. Test whether a list is an inventory, connected terms, contributions to one whole, supporting organisation or an operative capacity.
  3. Distinguish the physical bearer from the constitutive locus of completion. Distributed completion need not mean multiple bearers or actual encounters.
  4. Distinguish composition that makes a whole sufficient from dependence that is how it holds and operation that is what it accomplishes.
  5. Use cross-sentence comparison to reveal an inconsistency, then verify the new Centre against its own source. Do not copy a cell from a comparison.
  6. If a frozen Centre has omitted a governing achievement, create a new revision rather than arguing indefinitely within its artificially restricted candidates.

This is a case-derived diligence correction under the current protocol, not a change to canonical definitions or a universal rule that enumerated content selects Cell 40.
