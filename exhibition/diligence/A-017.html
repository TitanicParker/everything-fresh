Gate One — source analysis

A market price gathers weather, labor, distance, scarcity, and desire into one figure.

Unfolding and candidates

A market price is identified. “Gathers” makes the following plurality belong together in a result. Weather, labor, distance, scarcity and desire supply distinct contributions. “Into one figure” specifies the unity accomplished.

  1. Prices are affected by several things. Preserves influence but omits gathering into one figure.
  2. The five factors have equal numerical weights in a price formula. Invents equality and a formula.
  3. A single market-price figure gathers weather, labor, distance, scarcity and desire into one expression. Preserves plurality and their unified expression.
  4. Price determines everyone's conduct. Invents consequential behaviour.

Candidate 3 is strongest. No exhaustive theory of price, exact weighting, efficient-market claim or guaranteed fairness is supplied.
