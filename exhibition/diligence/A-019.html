Gate One — source analysis

Someone repairing a bicycle restores more than motion; they restore tomorrow's possibilities.

Unfolding and candidates

Someone is repairing a bicycle. “Restores more than motion” says physical movement alone is an inadequate measure of what is recovered. “They restore tomorrow's possibilities” completes the practical significance: usable mobility returns as renewed future options.

“Restore” preserves recovery rather than wholly unprecedented creation. “Possibilities” does not assert tomorrow's actual journeys. No particular owner, fault, destination or successful future trip is specified.

  1. A bicycle moves again. Incomplete: omits the practical scope of the restoration.
  2. Repair creates entirely new future opportunities. Overstates novelty and loses restoration.
  3. Repairing the bicycle restores motion together with the future possibilities that recovered mobility makes available. Preserves repair and its expanded significance.
  4. A person will definitely travel to work tomorrow. Invents destination, beneficiary and actual performance.

Candidate 3 is strongest. The restoration is one practical recovery, not two unrelated benefits.

Four holding tests

  1. Identity: a practical recovery of mobility, including future possibilities; not just a repaired object or a promised actual journey.
  2. Dependencies: the restored options belong to recovered functioning. Merely remembering a repair or possessing an inert bicycle does not preserve this recovered availability.
  3. Operative continuation: yes; possibilities for tomorrow are expressly restored. Their availability is achieved while their exercise is not asserted.
  4. Minimal invalidation: retain mechanical motion while deny recovered practical possibilities; the expanded restoration is no longer achieved.

Achieved-identity discrimination

What has been established is: bicycle repair restores usable motion and thereby renews future possibilities.

What obtains through what is: renewed practical possibilities obtain through the bicycle's recovered functioning.

The dependence belongs to the account, but the governing retained achievement is the recovered ability to proceed. A detached repaired condition without available continuation omits the expressly expanded restoration. No future journey must actually occur.
