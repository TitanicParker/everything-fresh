Gate One — source analysis

A hospital at midnight holds emergencies, recoveries, farewells, and beginnings under one roof.

Unfolding and competing Centres

“A hospital at midnight” gives the place and time. “Holds” introduces what belongs within that setting. Emergencies, recoveries, farewells and beginnings articulate different situations, some moving in contrary directions. “Under one roof” completes their common gathering.

  1. A hospital contains events. Too general: loses the range and its particular gathering.
  2. A hospital resolves every emergency. Adds successful treatment and erases the other situations.
  3. At midnight, emergencies, recoveries, farewells and beginnings are gathered within one hospital. Preserves the setting, range and common place.
  4. All the situations form one coordinated medical procedure. Invents functional coordination and shared purpose.

Candidate 3 survives. “Beginnings” and “farewells” are not narrowed to births and deaths; those are plausible examples, not necessary literal equivalents. Midnight does not establish that these events occur only at night.
