Gate One — source analysis

Clouds do not consult calendars before bringing rain to waiting fields.

Unfolding and candidate Centres

Clouds are presented as not consulting calendars. The continuation specifies the relevant activity: bringing rain to fields that wait for it. The personification separates a natural arrival from a human timetable while retaining the demand for rain.

  1. Clouds cannot read. Too literal and incomplete; loses rainfall and waiting fields.
  2. Rain never arrives on a desired date. Too strong; independence from consultation does not exclude coincidence or predictability.
  3. Rain comes to waiting fields without being answerable to human calendar consultation. Preserves the natural arrival, human temporal frame and unmet authority of that frame.
  4. Rain has escaped a calendar that once controlled it. Invents former control and a change of regime.

Candidate 3 survives. “Waiting” need not attribute consciousness to fields; it locates rainfall against an agricultural expectation. No drought, harvest failure or eventual satisfaction of every waiting field is asserted.

Four holding tests

  1. Identity: the determined independence of rain-to-fields from human calendar consultation.
  2. Constitutive dependencies: both domains identify this independence, but neither a sustaining comparison nor a conditional support makes the rainfall obtain. The achievement is the distinction itself.
  3. Operative continuation: no new power to proceed, successful crop, liberated rain-making process or altered scheduling practice is achieved. No reader's practical lesson is imported.
  4. Minimal invalidation: require calendar consultation for rainfall's arrival; that changes the independence while retaining rain and dates.

Achieved-identity discrimination

What has been established is: bringing rain to waiting fields does not answer to calendar consultation.

What obtains through what is: the distinction is specified by setting natural arrival against the human scheduling frame.

The second states how the independence is identified. It does not establish that the result remains dependent on an ongoing comparison or a support supplied by calendars. The natural and calendrical domains stand distinguished; no former calendrical authority has been overcome.
