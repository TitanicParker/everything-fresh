Gate One — source analysis

The smallest village may contain disagreements as intricate as those of capitals.

Unfolding and candidate Centres

“The smallest village” brings an extreme of settlement scale into view. “May contain disagreements” asserts possibility rather than a universal fact or a particular occurrence. “As intricate as those of capitals” gives that possible complexity a comparative determination.

  1. Villages have disagreements. Omits modality and comparative intricacy.
  2. All villages have disagreements identical to capital disputes. Adds universality, actuality and identity of content.
  3. Disagreements in even the smallest village may match those of capitals in intricacy. Preserves possibility, extreme scale and the specified dimension of comparison.
  4. Small settlements cause more disagreement than cities. Invents causation and reverses the comparative determination.

Candidate 3 survives. Matching intricacy does not mean sharing subjects, number of parties or political importance. “May” does not newly grant a village an operative permission; it states possible complexity.
