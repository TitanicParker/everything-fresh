Gate One — source analysis

There are places where winter silence makes a distant footstep sound enormous.

Unfolding and candidate Centres

“There are places where” asserts existence of settings, not a rule about all places. “Winter silence” specifies the setting's acoustic character. A “distant footstep” supplies a small, remote occurrence. “Sound enormous” gives it an extraordinary perceived scale through that quiet setting.

  1. A footstep is audible. Too weak: omits its enormous impression and constitutive setting.
  2. Winter silence physically amplifies the footstep's emitted energy. Adds an acoustic mechanism and replaces appearance with a physical measurement.
  3. In some places a distant footstep has an enormous perceived scale through winter silence. Preserves existence, season, distance, perception and contextual determination.
  4. All winter places make every footstep deafening. Adds universality, frequency and literal intensity.

Candidate 3 survives. “Enormous” is not a decibel value or a claim that the footstep becomes physically large.

Four holding tests

  1. Identity: a contextually determined perceptual magnitude, not mere detectability or emitted power.
  2. Constitutive dependencies: winter silence gives the enormous impression its particular frame. Remove that bearing and this frame-constituted perception is no longer the same account.
  3. Operative continuation: a perceptual result is expressly supplied, but no new sensory capacity, changed future conduct or independently continuing acoustic function is achieved.
  4. Minimal invalidation: retain the distant footstep and setting but remove its enormous impression; alternatively replace the frame and any retained large impression would have a different stated constitution.

Achieved-identity discrimination

What has been established is: some winter settings give a distant footstep enormous perceived scale.

What obtains through what is: the footstep's enormous impression obtains through the quiet acoustic frame.

A bare fact that the footstep sounds large omits what that perceptual magnitude consists in here. The frame is not merely evidence for an independently fixed intensity. The achieved result is the appearance through its context, without implying physical amplification or a before/after episode of altered hearing.
