## 5. Execute Gate One before classification

Work from the sentence's completed achievement. Do not choose an anticipated cell and paraphrase the sentence to fit it.

1. Copy exact wording, ID and provenance. Preserve quantifiers, modality, negation, scope and qualifications.
2. Reconstruct the unfolding: what does each contribution leave unresolved, and what makes the whole enough?
3. Generate at least three serious candidate Centres. Reject summaries that omit governing contributions and explanations that invent mechanisms.
4. Apply deletion, alteration, narrowing, reversal, dependency and sufficiency-location tests. Say what each test actually establishes.
5. Answer all six questions:
   - What was unresolved?
   - What changed?
   - What made completion sufficient?
   - Where is sufficiency located?
   - What remains achieved?
   - What would invalidate it?
6. Complete the four holding tests:
   - Exact identity of the retained achievement.
   - Dependencies constitutive of that identity.
   - Any actually established operative continuation or capacity.
   - Smallest change defeating the achievement.
7. Perform the paired achieved-identity formulations:
   - **What has been established is ___.**
   - **What obtains through what is ___.**
8. Test whether either complete formulation omits something or invents a dependency. Pursue the difference before freezing.
9. Check coverage, minimality, non-invention and determinacy.
10. Preserve the complete Centre Confirmation Record.

Required holding statement:

> What remains achieved is ___; it holds as this achievement because ___; removing ___ would change or defeat it in the following way: ___.

Then declare exactly:

**OPERATIVE CENTRE CONFIRMED — SOURCE SENTENCE CLOSED.**

A physical event can be predicted, described or explained without the utterance asserting that it has just happened. Keep that distinction clear.

The owner compared:

> Every forest floor is a crowded marketplace of decay, nourishment, and renewal.

> A single seed carries instructions for negotiating soil, sunlight, insects, and time.

The first analysis recognised a whole in A-003 but let **“carries”** steer A-007 toward possession. That was an inadequately tested difference.

Expressed from their Centres:

- A-003: decay, nourishment and renewal together constitute the forest floor as a crowded, active whole.
- A-007: instructions for the named domains together constitute the seed's unified instructional endowment.

Both current readings select **40 — Role array: Integration × Distributed × Standing**.

**Apply the lesson, not a template:**

- Identify the complete governing achievement before privileging the grammatical verb.
- Distinguish inventory, connection, contributions to a whole, sustaining dependence and operative capacity.
- A single bearer does not automatically make completion Focal.
- Distributed completion does not require actual encounters or multiple physical bearers.
- Composition that completes a whole is not automatically its ongoing supporting dependence.
- Practical orientation does not automatically establish an accomplished capacity; conversely, an unexercised capacity need not have been executed.
- A comparison can expose inconsistent reasoning, but each revised Centre must still be supported by its own source.
- Do not classify every list as Integration or every “carries” sentence as a whole.

Read A-007 revision 02 for the preserved correction. Do not restart its old debate as though no revision happened.

## 8. Further reasoning disciplines

- A necessary term's deletion defeats many completions. It does not alone discriminate Standing from Bearing.
- “Because” can establish support, but the achieved identity must still decide the conjunction.
- “When,” “after” and “then” do not automatically establish a causal mechanism.
- Restoration does not automatically establish Repair: identify the criterion-relative fault.
- Negation, absence, movement or independence from a prerequisite does not automatically establish Release: identify the former governor and its loss of force.
- A singular subject does not alone establish Focal; several nouns do not alone establish Distributed.
- A performed effect may support Efficacy, but motion or change alone is insufficient.
- Canonical names are crystallizations. Do not invent a literal cycle for “Relational cycle” or physical equilibrium for “Pivoted balance.”
- Do not require an explicit detailed mechanism when the governing organisation is already intelligible. Do not add one to overcome missing evidence either.

The user prefers decisive, interested structural work to repetitive hedging. Explain the actual discriminant rather than appending a generic caveat to every sentence.
