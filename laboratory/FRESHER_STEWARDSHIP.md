# Fresher — delegated stewardship of the 200-sentence resolution programme

**Mandate — 20 October 2026.** The repository owner delegates to **Fresher**, when invoked in a session with authorised GitHub write access, responsibility for working through all 200 frozen corpus sentences **one sentence per run**, saving the complete evidence in this repository, and resuming from its persisted state. This is permission to commit corpus-resolution records directly to `main`, without seeking fresh approval for each ordinary record. **It does not grant a background execution service, change the GPT's access permissions, or authorise edits to canonical definitions or frozen sentences.** Fresher acts when invoked and equipped with working write tools.

## Source and governing method

- Read [THE_54_CONTROL.md](../THE_54_CONTROL.md), [OPERATIVE_CENTRE_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.md](../OPERATIVE_CENTRE_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.md), [WORKFLOW.md](../WORKFLOW.md), and the exact [Edition 1.1 workbook](../OPERATIVE_CENTRE_WORKBOOK_200_SENTENCES.md) on each fresh session as needed.
- Corpus A (§15): A-001 through A-100. Corpus B (§16): B-001 through B-100. The workbook sentences are immutable research inputs.
- Follow the *current* protocol, including independent Gate One, the six-question record, four holding tests, achieved-identity discrimination, full freeze and One-Way Threshold. Gate Two begins only from the frozen record and canonical reference. Eliminate structural rivals; retain `contested` where real rivals survive. Never force a cell or rewrite a frozen Centre to obtain one.
- Previous chats and unregistered exploratory outputs are not automatically completed corpus records. The durable repository ledger determines progress.

## One-at-a-time operating cycle

1. **Resume from GitHub.** Read `laboratory/RESOLUTION_REGISTER.md` and inspect files in `laboratory/resolutions/`. The next target is the lowest numbered sentence without a *complete persisted first-pass record*, in A-001 → A-100 → B-001 → B-100 order. Existing contested or abstained records count as completed first-pass assays; their unresolved questions remain on the diligence queue and can be revisited as distinct revisions. Do not overwrite old work.
2. **One sentence only.** Verify the target's exact workbook text and provenance. Complete and explain Gate One **before** consulting the cell lookup. Prepare a self-contained frozen record, explicitly closing the source. Then complete Gate Two using that record alone.
3. **Write a detailed, reviewable per-sentence file** at `laboratory/resolutions/A-001.md` etc. Include: source ID, exact text, date and protocol version/commit; unfolding; competing Centres and eliminations; deletion/alteration/narrowing/reversal; six-question confirmation; four holding tests and achieved-identity paired formulations; exact frozen Centre and holding statement; the literal One-Way Threshold; unified dimensions; cell (if determined) and nearest rivals; structural exclusion evidence; limitations or unresolved discriminants; confidence/status; assessor identity; links to prior/revision records. **Segregate source analysis from post-freeze classification** by section headings. Do not retroactively edit the frozen record.
4. **Persist to GitHub directly.** With authorised tools, create the sentence file on the default branch and only after that succeeds append its indexed entry to `laboratory/RESOLUTION_REGISTER.md` including a link to its full record and outcome. Verify both writes by reading them back. If the ledger update fails after the file succeeds, repair the ledger without resolving or duplicating the sentence. If no write tool/access is available, **do not claim persistence**: return the complete record and clearly say write access is needed.
5. **Publish the sentence receipt.** After the full research record and register entry have been saved and verified, add or update `sentence-receipts.html`: exact sentence, current cell and full conjunction (or surviving alternatives), status, and readable paragraphs explaining how the structure and canonical name show themselves. Link to `exhibition/diligence/{ID}.html`, rendered from the current full Markdown record, and refresh `RESOLUTION_REGISTER.html`. Update the public receipt on revisions while keeping earlier research records intact. Verify these public surfaces as part of completion. The owner expressly authorised this public receipt cycle on 20 October 2026; ordinary direct commits for these surfaces are included in stewardship.
6. **Report and stop.** State the sentence ID, achieved Centre, selected cell or unresolved rivalry, permanent repository record URL, ledger confirmation, and next target. Do **not** claim that the next sentence was processed or promise background progress. Continue with the next sentence only on the next invocation or a separately authorised running workflow.

## Audit trail and revision discipline

- First-pass record = `laboratory/resolutions/{ID}.md`; keep it immutable once committed. If a subsequent study changes the result, add `laboratory/resolutions/{ID}-revision-02.md`, `-revision-03.md`, etc., referencing previous work, and append an index entry. Never erase an earlier result.
- Record `candidate`, `contested`, or `abstained` honestly. A contested sentence is **not skipped** or marked unworked; the preserved Centre and unresolved cell boundary are research evidence.
- Preserve exact source text, prompts or instruction versions, assessor/date, rival exclusions, and decisive tests for diligence. No invented classifications or completed-work claims.
- To resolve a race or an interrupted write, check actual repository files first; treat GitHub as authority over conversation memory. Do not make parallel edits to the single register.
- **Scope of direct-write permission:** sentence records, revisions, their index entries, and the corresponding public receipts, rendered diligence and HTML register updates described above. Keep these public surfaces aligned with current indexed results. Do not silently change `THE_54_CONTROL.md`, either frozen corpus, the working protocol, or unrelated exhibition content. Propose substantive methodological or canonical revisions separately for owner review.
- Use direct commits for authorised corpus records; pull requests remain appropriate for changes outside that scope.
