## Gate One — source analysis and Centre confirmation

### Source boundary and unfolding

Exact frozen source (Corpus A, §15, item 1):

> The morning sun reaches mountain villages long before their valleys receive warmth.

The source is an ordinary general statement. Its generic plural is retained without adding a universal claim about every village or a measurement of the interval. Sunlight reaching villages and warmth reaching their own valleys are different specified arrivals. No account of terrain, temperature, thermal causation or the sun's physical mechanism is supplied.

1. “The morning sun” establishes the morning setting and source.
2. “reaches mountain villages” establishes an arrival, leaving its comparison with the valleys unresolved.
3. “long before” introduces substantial precedence, requiring the later endpoint.
4. “their valleys receive warmth” supplies that endpoint and the village–own-valley pairing. The whole establishes the substantial ordered separation.

### Deletion, alteration, narrowing, reversal and dependency tests

| Test | Variation and exact consequence |
|---|---|
| Deletion of comparison | Retain only the first arrival: an arrival report survives, but no substantial lead. Survival of a grammatical clause does not establish its priority over the original whole. |

### Four sharpened holding tests

1. **Identity.** What remains achieved is the substantial temporal ordering itself, including the specified arrivals and their pairing. It is neither an isolated arrival nor an independent capacity.
2. **Constitutive dependencies.** Change the endpoints, direction or substantial interval and the identity of this ordering changes. These are the content of the ordered configuration. That necessity does not by itself establish that the identity of either occurrence is produced or sustained through the other.
3. **Operative continuation.** No changed continuation, practical capacity or prescribed way of proceeding is established. Nor is a causal heating relationship asserted.
4. **Minimal invalidation.** Simultaneity eliminates precedence while leaving both arrivals intact. Removing substantial separation alone defeats the specifically substantial lead but preserves a weaker ordering. The first defeats the order; the second changes its degree.

### Achieved-identity discrimination

**Result formulation:** What has been established is a substantial temporal ordering of morning sunlight at mountain villages before warmth in their own valleys.

**Constitution formulation:** What obtains through what is the village arrival's temporal priority relative to the later arrival of valley warmth.

**Completion versus constitution.** The latter names a position within the completed ordering. The complete result formulation already retains that priority. Nothing further is lost when the whole achievement is described as the ordered configuration itself.

**Identity rather than prerequisites.** The paired endpoints, morning setting, direction and interval specify which order has been established. Their indispensability is acknowledged; it is not used as an automatic argument that a further dependent identity has been accomplished.

**Further pre-freeze tests.**

- Retain both occurrences but erase their order: the achievement is lost.
- Retain the full substantial order but omit a separate statement about “acquired earliness”: nothing governing is lost; that statement duplicates a position in the order.
- Change either timing: the configuration changes. No separately established function, prominence, authority or capacity is shown to change through it.
- Deny that one arrival causes the other while preserving their timing: the achievement survives; a causal dependence is unnecessary.

**Discrimination outcome.** The complete ordered configuration is the retained achievement. The “through” formulation is adequate as a redescription of its positions, but supplies no independently supported difference in achieved identity. This does not require that every dependence be causal; the test concerns whether a different constitutive achievement has actually been identified. Residual interpretive limits must be reported, not hidden by paraphrase.
