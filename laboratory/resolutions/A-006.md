## Gate One — source analysis and freeze

### Exact source and unfolding

> The ocean moves heat between continents, quietly shaping weather and harvests.

**Source:** A-006, Corpus A §15, Edition 1.1 workbook, original item 6; source file `One_Hundred_Statements_About_the_World(4).md`; workbook blob `1b511a439a5007a3c4a61c36ff5417f415005e2c`. Text verified verbatim.

The opening introduces the ocean as an agent of heat movement. “Between continents” specifies a span of redistribution rather than merely local heat movement. The following contribution establishes consequential influence on weather and harvests. “Quietly” presents that influence as unobtrusive; it does not supply a measured sound level or assert that nobody recognises it.

The completed point is the ocean's unobtrusive consequence for weather and harvests through its intercontinental movement of heat. The weather/harvest contribution is not an unrelated second report. No named current, route, temperature change, crop, benefit, exclusive cause or quantified effect is specified.

### Four sharpened holding tests

1. **Identity:** An unobtrusive operative influence on weather and harvests through oceanic heat movement between continents. Neither the ocean's existence nor a transport inventory alone is the achieved point.
2. **Constitutive dependencies:** Source, transported heat, intercontinental span and specified consequences identify this influence. The dependence explains its means, but the retained achievement includes what that means actually does.
3. **Operative continuation:** Yes. Heat redistribution is presented as consequential for weather and harvests. This is explicit influence within the asserted content, not a practical use inferred from the statement.
4. **Minimal invalidation:** Retain movement but deny all specified effects: the transport remains, while the achieved consequential connection fails. Removing a domain of influence instead changes coverage, not necessarily all efficacy.

### Achieved-identity discrimination

**Result formulation:** What has been established is that oceanic heat movement between continents unobtrusively influences weather and harvests.

**Constitution formulation:** What obtains through what is the influence on weather and harvests through the ocean's intercontinental heat transport.

Both preserve the whole, but neither should be weakened to an arrangement of related terms. The source's completed result includes consequential influence. A transport–effect dependence is indispensable; what remains achieved is that dependence in operation, rather than only the identity of a condition-held relation.

Further pre-freeze tests:

- Keep a map of transport and effect domains but deny that the transport influences them: descriptive connections remain; the achievement fails.
- Retain influence but omit a detailed physical pathway: nothing essential is lost, because none was specified.
- Retain consequential influence while deny a closed feedback loop: nothing essential is lost; no return path was asserted.
- Retain the intercontinental operation but treat the ocean as one physical entity: the single source does not erase the constitutive span.
- Remove any assumption of benefit, equilibrium or fault correction: the assertion remains complete.

**Outcome:** The identity is the consequential reach of the specified transport. No mechanism beyond the presented influence is needed. Source identity and spatial span both matter, but completion is recorded across the transport and consequences rather than at an isolated source.
