## Gate One — independent re-assay with disclosed exposure

### Exact source and unfolding

> A single seed carries instructions for negotiating soil, sunlight, insects, and time.

A-007, Corpus A §15, Edition 1.1 workbook, original item 7; source file `One_Hundred_Statements_About_the_World(4).md`; verified verbatim against workbook blob `1b511a439a5007a3c4a61c36ff5417f415005e2c`.

“A single seed” establishes the particular carrier and emphasises its small unitary scale. “Carries instructions” attributes action-directed information, not merely unspecified contents. “For negotiating” gives that information prospective orientation. The four domains complete its scope. Literal verbal directions, intentional understanding, a specified genetic mechanism and successful execution are not asserted.

### Candidate Centres

1. A seed contains information: incomplete, because the information's practical orientation and domains disappear.
2. One seed carries guidance for negotiating the four specified conditions: complete at the level of content attribution.
3. One seed is equipped by its carried guidance to negotiate those conditions: a plausible stronger reading; it may express the practical force of instructions, but may add effective usability.
4. The seed successfully survives every condition: unsupported success and universal outcome.
5. The four conditions are coordinated into one ecological system: adds an organisation of the conditions not established by their naming.

### Four holding tests

1. **Identity:** one seed's possession of action-directed guidance across the named domains. Whether effective preparedness is part of that identity remains the exact open issue.
2. **Constitutive dependencies:** the seed, scoped content and prospective orientation are indispensable. Possession may be retained as an attribution or interpreted as the endowment constituted by carried guidance.
3. **Operative continuation:** actual execution and success are absent. The phrase's practical orientation supports a possible unexercised endowment; it must not be rejected merely for lack of execution, or asserted solely from the label “instructions.”
4. **Minimal invalidation:** replace guidance with irrelevant information: the scoped attribution fails. Disable all possible operative influence while retaining guidance content: the preparedness reading fails, but the attribution reading may remain.

### Achieved-identity discrimination and further tests

**Result formulation:** What has been established is that one seed carries guidance for negotiating soil, sunlight, insects and time.

**Constitution formulation:** What obtains through what is the seed's prospective preparedness for these conditions through the guidance it carries.

The first preserves complete attribution; the second captures practical orientation but may add an effective endowment. Further tests before freezing do not select uniquely: possession without execution preserves both; possession without usability separates them but leaves the source metaphor's application disputed; removing practical orientation defeats both. A simple possession-through-content formulation also preserves a possible constitutive identity, without independently discriminating it from the established attribution.

**Outcome:** do not freeze “no capacity established” as a settled finding. Freeze the scoped guidance with the possession/endowment ambiguity explicit. A single source interpretation cannot be manufactured by choosing the word “attribution” or “capacity.”
