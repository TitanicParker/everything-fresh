## Gate One — source analysis and freeze

### Exact source and unfolding

> A grandmother's recipe survives because somebody remembers what cannot be written down.

A-010, frozen Corpus A §15, Edition 1.1, original item 10; source file `One_Hundred_Statements_About_the_World(4).md`; source verified against workbook blob `1b511a439a5007a3c4a61c36ff5417f415005e2c`.

The opening identifies a particular inherited recipe and establishes its survival, leaving the stated support unresolved. “Because somebody remembers” supplies an actual preserving contribution. “What cannot be written down” specifies the remembered contribution as knowledge not fully available in written form. The complete achievement is the recipe's continued availability through remembered unwritable knowledge.

The source does not identify the person, say that the grandmother is dead, assert that a written recipe exists, specify the remembered content or assert that cooking is performed. “Cannot” is preserved rather than weakened into “has not yet.” It need not imply that all aspects of the recipe are unwritable.

### Four holding tests

1. **Identity:** this recipe's continued availability through remembered knowledge that cannot be captured in writing. Mere survival of a text or title is not sufficient for this particular supported preservation.
2. **Constitutive dependencies:** remembering is an actual preserving support and the remembered content is unwritable. These remain part of how the recipe survives here, rather than only prerequisites for describing an independent relic.
3. **Operative continuation:** no specific act of cooking, accomplished performance or new capacity is asserted. Continued availability is retained through support; it is not inferred from the practical usefulness of recipes.
4. **Minimal invalidation:** remove the remembered unwritable contribution while leave a name or document: the asserted mode of preservation is defeated. The claim does not entail sole-cause extinction if other support were introduced.

### Achieved-identity discrimination

**Result formulation:** What has been established is that a grandmother's recipe remains available because someone remembers its unwritable knowledge.

**Constitution formulation:** What obtains through what is the recipe's continued availability through somebody's memory of what writing cannot retain.

The complete result formulation itself retains an operative supporting dependency. Reduce it to “the recipe is preserved as an established object” and the governing achievement loses how that preservation holds. The constitution formulation captures that retained support, rather than simply adding a relation to an independently completed result.

Further tests before freezing: written remnants without the remembered contribution do not preserve this support-held achievement; unrelated memory does not suffice; retaining the remembering but omitting actual cooking preserves the assertion; adding a second possible source of preservation would not refute that the named memory actually supports survival. Thus support is governing, while performed operation and exclusivity are not.
