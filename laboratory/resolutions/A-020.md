### Unfolding and candidates

The completed point holds variation in division together with recognition of the familiar. It neither makes language an impermeable prison nor asserts that all speakers divide experience alike. “Familiar” does not establish identical interpretations, identical referents for every speaker, or universal mutual translation.

1. Languages classify experience differently. Omits recognition.
2. All languages express exactly the same meanings. Contradicts or flattens the stated differences.

5. Language has newly given people a capacity they previously lacked. Adds a before/after acquisition.

Candidate 3 is strongest. The helping function is retained within the contrast; it is not denied because the whole concerns their coexistence.
