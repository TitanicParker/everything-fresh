## Gate One — source analysis

> The bee visits a flower, and an orchard's future changes almost invisibly.

### Unfolding and candidate Centres

A bee visits a flower. The continuation extends that small occurrence into a change in an orchard's future. “Almost invisibly” qualifies how unobtrusively the change occurs.

1. A bee visits a flower. Loses the consequence and scale.
2. Two unrelated events happen near one another. Grammatically possible but leaves their completed point unexplained.
3. The bee's flower visit has an almost imperceptible consequence for the orchard's future. Preserves the local occurrence's consequential reach.
4. The bee guarantees a successful orchard harvest through a specified pollination mechanism. Adds outcome, guarantee and detailed mechanism.

Candidate 3 is selected as the ordinary unified reading. The conjunction alone is not a general rule proving causation: this reading rests on the utterance presenting the orchard-future change as the significance of the small visit. The record does not strengthen that into a complete biological explanation. If only unrelated conjunction were intended, that would be a different Centre.

### Four holding tests

1. **Identity:** an almost imperceptible consequential difference extending from a local occurrence to a larger future.
2. **Constitutive dependencies:** the visit and future-change identify the consequence. The record does not claim the orchard's entire future depends exclusively on the bee.
3. **Operative continuation:** yes; a change to the future is the express completed point, not a reader's optional application.
4. **Minimal invalidation:** preserve the visit while deny any difference to that future.

### Achieved-identity discrimination

**What has been established is:** the visit is consequential for the orchard's future in an almost imperceptible way.

**What obtains through what is:** a difference to that future occurs through the visit's influence.

The result is not merely an established link or a future described through a static counterpart. Its identity is the difference the small occurrence makes. No particular benefit or measurable harvest is required.
