## Gate One — source analysis

> There are places where winter silence makes a distant footstep sound enormous.

### Unfolding and candidate Centres

“There are places where” asserts existence of settings, not a rule about all places. “Winter silence” specifies the setting's acoustic character. A “distant footstep” supplies a small, remote occurrence. “Sound enormous” gives it an extraordinary perceived scale through that quiet setting.

1. A footstep is audible. Too weak: omits its enormous impression and constitutive setting.
2. Winter silence physically amplifies the footstep's emitted energy. Adds an acoustic mechanism and replaces appearance with a physical measurement.
3. In some places a distant footstep has an enormous perceived scale through winter silence. Preserves existence, season, distance, perception and contextual determination.
4. All winter places make every footstep deafening. Adds universality, frequency and literal intensity.

Candidate 3 survives. “Enormous” is not a decibel value or a claim that the footstep becomes physically large.
