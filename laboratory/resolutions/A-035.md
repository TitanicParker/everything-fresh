## Gate One — source investigation (pre-taxonomic)

> The same rain nourishes one field and washes another field away.

**Unfolding:** “The same rain” fixes one common occurrence, not two different rainfalls. “Nourishes one field” establishes one beneficial outcome, but the governing point is still incomplete. “And washes another field away” supplies an opposed, destructive outcome in another field. The complete point is the divergence of consequences under one shared rain. “Another” is indispensable: the outcomes are not asserted of the same field. No soil, drainage, rainfall intensity, timing or moral explanation is stated.

**Three candidate Centres:**
1. *Rain affects fields.* Rejected as incomplete: it omits the same rain and the opposite consequences.
2. *The same rain has a beneficial consequence in one field and a destructive consequence in another.* Retained: minimal complete governing determination.
3. *Different land management makes rain beneficial in one field and disastrous in another.* Rejected: invents an explanatory cause.
4. *Rain is intrinsically good and bad.* Rejected: makes an unsupported general judgement instead of preserving the distinct field outcomes.

**Controlled tests:** Deleting “same” removes the common occurrence; separate rainfalls could then account for the outcomes and the particular contrast is lost. Deleting either field/outcome leaves only one consequence and loses the divergence. Replacing “nourishes” with “washes away” removes opposition; replacing “washes away” with “nourishes” likewise. Narrowing the claim to one field loses the across-field contrast. Reversing the field assignments retains a common source and opposed consequences but changes which field receives which outcome; the original identifies no fields independently, so this is a symmetry rather than evidence of causal direction. Replacing the second field with the first changes the two-location organisation. The outcomes require both the common rain and the differentiated recipients; neither alone completes the point. These tests establish indispensability, not a hidden physical mechanism or a particular taxonomy.

### Six confirmation questions

- **Unresolved:** What consequences the same rain has across the fields is not yet determined by the initial phrase.
- **Changed:** The whole establishes that its consequences diverge: nourishment here, destruction there.
- **Sufficient through:** A shared rain occurrence together with two differently affected fields and explicitly opposed outcomes.
- **Location of sufficiency:** Across the paired field outcomes under the common rain; neither outcome alone is sufficient.
- **What remains achieved:** One rain occurrence is understood as producing opposed consequences across two fields.
- **Minimal invalidation:** Make the two consequences alike, or remove the common-rain identity; either defeats the particular shared-source divergence.

### Holding tests and identity discrimination

**Identity:** The established achievement is a common occurrence's divergent, consequential treatment of two fields. **Constitutive dependency:** The sameness of the rain and opposition of effects are part of what the achievement is, not merely evidence for it. **Operative continuation:** The outcomes are expressly consequential—one field is nourished, the other washed away. No future yield, death, erosion mechanism or later recovery is asserted. **Minimal invalidation:** If the second outcome ceased to oppose the first, the achieved divergence would disappear even though rain still affected both.

**Result formulation:** What has been established is the opposed consequences of one rain occurrence across two fields.

**Constitution formulation:** What obtains through what is the difference in the fields' outcomes through their exposure to the same rain.

Both formulations retain the shared occurrence and opposing effects. The latter highlights the common condition, but does not establish that a field's identity is continuously sustained by a counterpart. The former risks sounding merely descriptive unless the actual nourishment and washing-away are explicitly retained. The positive identity must retain the *achieved effects*, not only a contrast in labels.

**Positive holding statement:** What remains achieved is one rain's opposed consequences across two fields; it holds as this achievement because the same occurrence nourishes one while washing the other away; removing either the shared occurrence or the opposition of consequences defeats this particular divergence.
