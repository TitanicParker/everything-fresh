# A-041 — dialogue resolution, 23 October 2026

**Sentence:** In a courtroom, silence can carry more weight than a long speech.

**Selected cell:** **04 — Scope standing — “Within this.”** (Differentiation × Distributed × Standing).

**Operative Centre:** The opening **“In a courtroom”** governs the scope within which silence can carry more weight than a long speech. The comparison is what holds within that specified domain, rather than the governing operation itself.

**Gate One:** The opening delimits the setting. Removing “In a courtroom” leaves a grammatically possible comparison, but removes the stated contextual determination. The operative sentence establishes a context-specific possibility, not an actual judgment, verdict, or settlement. “Can” preserves possibility rather than asserting an adjudication.

**Gate Two:** **Differentiation** — the courtroom distinguishes the relevant domain. **Distributed** — the scope governs the comparative alternatives, silence and long speech, together. **Standing** — the sentence states what can hold within that domain. These are aspects of one contextual achievement, not three independent lexical cues.
