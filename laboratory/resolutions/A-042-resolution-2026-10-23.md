# A-042 — Coral reef: sheltering governs nested relationships

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Sentence:** “The coral reef shelters creatures whose survival depends upon countless close relationships.”  
**Owner-selected resolution:** **Cell 19 — Direct bearing — “It bears on this.”**  
**Conjunction:** **Relating × Focal × Standing**.  
**Status:** accepted in dialogue; recorded owner-led resolution, not a claim of independent uniqueness proof.

## Gate One — Operative Centre

**Operative Centre:** “The coral reef shelters creatures.” The sentence makes plain **what the coral reef does**. The subject–verb–object structure gives the governing assertion: the reef shelters creatures. “Whose survival depends upon countless close relationships” is a relative clause modifying *creatures*. It tells us about the creatures' conditions of survival; it does not replace the reef's sheltering as the operative achievement. The relationships are nested within the governing structure, not coequal centres.

**Control test:** removing the relative clause leaves the governing assertion intact: “The coral reef shelters creatures.” Removing the sheltering assertion leaves only a dependent description of creatures and their survival, not the sentence's principal achievement. This grammatical test supports, but does not alone prove, the Centre choice.

## Gate Two — coherent dimensional reading

- **Relating:** the sentence establishes the reef's sheltering relation to creatures, rather than primarily contrasting kinds or narrating a repair.
- **Focal:** the governing assertion is one sheltering achievement. “Countless” modifies nested relationships and does not distribute operative governance across them.
- **Standing:** sheltering is presented as what the reef does, a standing ecological function, not as a single event newly completed in this utterance.

The three dimensions describe the same achievement: the reef **bears directly on** the sheltered creatures' lives. Thus **Cell 19 — Direct bearing**.

## Serious rival and disposition

**Distributed topology / a relationships-centred reading:** “countless close relationships” could tempt the analyst to take multiplicity as the centre. But the relationships occur inside a subordinate relative clause describing creatures, whereas the reef's sheltering is asserted by the main clause. Quantity within the explanation is not operative distribution. The rival therefore does not dislodge the focal sheltering Centre.

**Provenance:** The owner first selected Cell 19 with “the coral reef shelters” as Centre, then clarified: “The point of this sentence is to make plain what the coral reef does, the countless relationships are nested within this governing structure.” This receipt records that accepted analysis; it does not claim a blind uniqueness test.
