# A-043 — dialogue working resolution, 22 October 2026

**Sentence:** Every public library offers strangers access to work completed by absent minds.

**Selected candidate:** **51 — Case settlement — “Settled across the cases.”** (Settlement × Distributed × Efficacy).

**Traffic cop:** “Every … offers”.

**Operative Centre:** Exhaustive coverage of the claim across the class of public libraries: no member of that class is excepted.

**Three axes together:** Settlement is proposed as the exhaustive determination; Distributed is the universal range of libraries; Efficacy is the proposed accomplished coverage across cases. Caution: the sentence asserts a universal fact but does not explicitly narrate a prior unsettled question or the process of settling cases. This is a dialogue-selected working classification, not a proof of uniqueness.

**Rival:** **15 — Field distribution — “Available throughout.”** (Opening × Distributed × Efficacy). “Offers access” asserts availability; “every” may distribute that opening rather than accomplish settlement. “A public library offers access” removes universal scope but preserves the opening; “Every public library has a roof” preserves scope but removes the opening. These tests remain relevant.

**Do not infer:** an actual visitor, a library transaction, a prior dispute, or a decision-maker.
