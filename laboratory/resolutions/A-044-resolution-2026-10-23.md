# A-044 — owner-selected resolution

Sentence: A lost key changes the meaning of an otherwise ordinary locked door.

Selected: Cell 19 — Direct bearing (Relating × Focal × Standing).

Operative Centre: “A lost key changes the meaning.” The lost key changes how the locked door is understood. “Otherwise ordinary” supplies the contrast, not a second governing operation.

Gate One: Removing “otherwise ordinary” leaves the principal bearing intact, but loses its baseline. Removing “lost” loses the specified reason the key affects the interpretation. The sentence does not claim that the door changes physically.

Gate Two: Relating — the lost key bears on the door's significance. Focal — one governing effect. Standing — a stated relation rather than a completed verdict.

Rivals: Differentiation highlights the ordinary-versus-altered contrast but misses the governing bearing. Settlement would import a final interpretation not stated.

Provenance: owner-selected dialogue resolution. This records a selection, not independent proof of uniqueness.
