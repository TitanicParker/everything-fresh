# A-046 — selected dialogue resolution

Sentence: A farmer reads the soil through texture, smell, memory, and experience.

Cell 46 — Settled alternative — Chosen.
Settlement × Focal × Standing.

Operative Centre: the farmer's established way of reading soil through texture, smell, memory, and experience. The methods are already chosen.

Settlement: the method is established, not chosen in the sentence. Focal: the inputs serve one act of reading. Standing: the practice holds as established.

Deletion test: without the phrase beginning through, reading remains, but its specified method disappears.

Rivals: Integration × Focal emphasises convergence of inputs. Settlement × Release × Bearing would require a completed interpretation the sentence does not assert. Distributed would mistake several means for several governing achievements.

The judge analogy distinguishes an established method from the explicit completion of a verdict. This is an owner-selected dialogue resolution, not independent proof of uniqueness. The canonical description of Cell 46 in terms of a definitive answer remains a question for later methodological review.
