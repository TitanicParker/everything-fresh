# A-050 — owner-selected Cell 22

**Sentence:** A treaty is paper until people organize their conduct around its promises.

**Operative Centre:** The sentence establishes an ordered dependency: a treaty remains merely paper until its promises become a basis for human conduct. It states the condition of practical standing, not an event that necessarily already occurred.

**Cell 22 — Sequential link: Relating × Distributed × Standing.** Relating: treaty's significance depends upon human conduct. Distributed: *until* joins two ordered conditions (paper alone; conduct organized around promises); the sequence is constitutive, not merely a word-trigger. Standing: a general conditional relation is established, without claiming a particular treaty has completed the transition.

**Rivals:** Cell 21 (Relating × Focal × Efficacy) emphasizes practical effect but overreads the sentence as achieved efficacy. Cell 19 (Relating × Focal × Standing) captures dependency but underplays the ordered distribution across two conditions. Both remain documented as rivals, not chosen.

**Owner's decisive test:** “when we hear the word until, we think sequence and when we think sequence, I think it's cell 22 then.” Accepted in dialogue; owner-selected, not independently proven unique.
