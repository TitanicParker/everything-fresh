# A-053 — Cell 08, Graded contrast

**Sentence:** An unanswered message can govern an afternoon more strongly than a ringing bell.

**Gate One — Operative Centre:** “can govern more strongly than.” The sentence identifies two items and establishes that one can have a greater degree of influence over an afternoon than the other. The comparative achievement, not the mere existence of either influence, governs the utterance.

**Gate Two — Cell 08: Differentiation × Release × Bearing.** Canonical label: **Graded contrast**; cue **“More and less.”** Differentiation: unanswered message versus ringing bell. Release: the comparison carries the assessment beyond either item considered in isolation to an open comparative scale; this is the least independently secure dimension and should not be inferred from comparison alone. Bearing: the sentence specifies relative influence upon an afternoon. The modal “can” states a possibility, not an invariant outcome.

**Rival tests:** Cell 04 (Scope standing) and Cell 06 (Field separation) do not as directly capture the sentence's graded “more strongly than” determination. Relating is not compelled merely by comparison: the message and bell need not affect each other. The owner explicitly accepted Cell 08 because two items are identified and one is said to be more than the other.
