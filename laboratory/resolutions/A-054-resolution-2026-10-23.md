# A-054 — Cell 22, Sequential link

**Sentence:** The classroom becomes quieter when the difficult idea finally finds a clear example.

**Owner-accepted resolution:** **Cell 22 — Relating × Distributed × Standing, Sequential link (“Then”).**

**Operative Centre:** The classroom becomes quieter **when** the difficult idea finally finds a clear example. **“When”** is the structural operator establishing the temporal link between the idea finding an example and the classroom becoming quieter. The comparative “quieter” indicates a change but does not govern the sentence's centre. The two developments are related through their sequence/temporal ordering.
