# A-055 — Cell 02, Binary bearing

**Sentence:** A compass points north, but travelers must still decide where to go.

**Owner-selected resolution:** **Cell 02 — Differentiation × Focal × Bearing, Binary bearing (“This, not that”).**

**Operative Centre:** **The travelers, not the compass, must decide where to go.** The sentence locates responsibility for deciding with the travelers rather than the compass. “But” introduces the contrast; “must still” makes the travelers' obligation persist despite the compass's directional information.

**Single-origin three-axis test:** The three axes must emerge simultaneously from this one determination, not from unrelated fragments of the sentence. **Differentiation:** the deciding agent is travelers, not compass. **Focal:** the responsibility is assigned to the travelers as one locus. **Bearing:** the distinction determines where responsibility for the decision lies. Earlier analysis had improperly harvested Differentiation from “but”, Focal from travelers, and Bearing from “must still” as separate observations. The revised account unifies them in the one operative assignment of decision-making responsibility.
