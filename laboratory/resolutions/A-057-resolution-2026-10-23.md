# A-057 — Cell 52, Transformation release

**Sentence:** The old harbor became a park, and the city's edge changed purpose.

**Owner-accepted resolution:** Cell 52 — Settlement × Release × Standing, Transformation release (“Still itself”).

**Operative Centre:** **The old harbor became a park.** The verb “became” establishes a before-and-after transformation of the same site. What was a harbor is now a park. The second clause, “and the city's edge changed purpose,” echoes and reinforces the change of standing rather than necessarily constituting a separate sequential event.

**Single-origin axes:** Settlement: the change is presented as accomplished. Release: the place passes beyond its former harbor identity and purpose. Standing: the statement establishes the site's changed condition, not a mechanism or productive work. The place persists through a change of use (“Still itself”).

**Rivals examined:** Cell 22 (Sequential link) was considered because of before-and-after change, but the sentence does not require two sequentially related events. Cell 48 (Path completion, Settlement × Focal × Efficacy) was proposed and withdrawn: change of purpose is not by itself an efficacy or mechanism. Cell 46 (Settled alternative) does not capture transformation as precisely as Cell 52.

**Status:** owner-selected and directed to record; not independently proven unique. **Next:** A-058 — A telescope extends sight without removing the need to interpret what appears.
