# A-058 — Cell 18, Edge opening

**Sentence:** A telescope extends sight without removing the need to interpret what appears.

**Owner-accepted resolution (23 October 2026):** **Cell 18 — Opening × Release × Efficacy, Edge opening.**

**Operative Centre (Gate One):** **A telescope extends sight.** The sentence establishes an increase in visual reach made possible by the telescope. The qualification “without removing the need to interpret what appears” marks what the extension does *not* accomplish; it does not supply the central operation or an independent source for an axis.

**Single-origin axes (Gate Two):** **Opening:** the telescope makes farther objects available to sight. **Release:** an earlier limit of unaided visual reach loses its determining force for what can be seen, without implying that all limits vanish. **Efficacy:** the telescope accomplishes the extension. All three describe the same operative achievement.

**Rivals investigated:** Differentiation × Focal × Efficacy (Cell 03) was considered but would force extension into a boundary-defining act; the sentence chiefly opens access. Opening × Focal × Efficacy (Cell 12) was considered because the telescope is a focal instrument, but its branching-output reading is weaker than the release of visual reach beyond a prior limit. Opening × Distributed × Efficacy (Cell 15) lacks a constitutively distributed achievement. The qualifying interpretation clause does not relocate the Centre.

**Status:** owner-confirmed, directed to record; a reasoned classification, **not** a claim of independently proven uniqueness. **Next in numerical sequence:** A-059; **A-056 remains unrecorded**, and A-047 remains a recorded dual-candidate case.
